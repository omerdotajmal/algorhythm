Decrease & Conquer

● 1 available

Reduce the problem to a single smaller instance of itself, solve that, then extend the solution back. Simpler than divide & conquer — no merging step required.

Decrease & Conquer Sorting

Insertion Sort

Grow a sorted sub-array one element at a time — pick the next element and shift larger elements right until the correct position is found.

Open → O(n²)
